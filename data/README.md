# Data

The radial-velocity data lives in `data/raw/`. All three files are RadVel's
own example datasets (MIT licence), small enough to commit, and needed by
`tests/test_rv.py` and the RV experiments. The table below records where each
file came from, so anyone can download it again and check it.

## Datasets used

| File | System | Source / citation | Downloaded by | Date |
|------|--------|-------------------|---------------|------|
| `k2-24.csv` (renamed from `epic203771098.csv`) | K2-24 (EPIC 203771098), a two-planet sub-Saturn system | 32 Keck/HIRES RV points (columns `t`, `vel`, `errvel`). This is the dataset of RadVel's ["K2-24 Fitting+MCMC" tutorial](https://radvel.readthedocs.io/en/latest/tutorials/K2-24_Fitting%2BMCMC.html), the one named in our proposal. Source file: [`example_data/epic203771098.csv`](https://github.com/California-Planet-Search/radvel/blob/master/example_data/epic203771098.csv) in the RadVel repository. The system was discovered by Petigura et al. 2016 (ApJ 818, 36), with RV masses in Petigura et al. 2018 (AJ 156, 89); cite both for the system itself. | Fariha | 2026-09-11 |
| `hd164922.txt` (renamed from `164922_fixed.txt`) | HD 164922, a four-planet system | 401 RV points over about 7,000 days from three instruments, one per value of the `tel` column (columns `time`, `mnvel`, `errvel`, `tel`, `svalue`): `k`, Keck/HIRES before its August 2004 detector upgrade (52 points, 1996-2004); `j`, HIRES after the upgrade (276 points, 2004-2015); and `a`, the Levy spectrograph on the Automated Planet Finder at Lick Observatory (73 points, 2014-2015). This matches the `k`, `j`, `a` instruments in RadVel's `example_planets/HD164922.py` and the HIRES and APF data of Fulton et al. 2016 (ApJ 830, 46). Source file: [`example_data/164922_fixed.txt`](https://github.com/California-Planet-Search/radvel/blob/master/example_data/164922_fixed.txt). Planets and discovery references (NASA Exoplanet Archive): b, Butler et al. 2006, P≈1207 d, e≈0.05; c, Fulton et al. 2016, P≈75.8 d, e≈0.22; d, Benatti et al. 2020, P≈12.5 d, e≈0.12; e, Rosenthal et al. 2021, P≈41.8 d, e≈0.09. It is the larger, multi-instrument alternative to K2-24 (401 points against 32). `rv.radvel_bridge.build_posterior` gives each instrument its own zero point and jitter. | Fariha | 2026-09-13 |
| `k2-131.txt` | K2-131, a single-planet system | 70 RV points from two instruments, HARPS-N (39) and PFS (31) (columns `time`, `mnvel`, `errvel`, `tel`). Source file: [`example_data/k2-131.txt`](https://github.com/California-Planet-Search/radvel/blob/master/example_data/k2-131.txt). K2-131 b has an ultra-short period (P≈0.3693 d) and is reported as tidally circularised (e≈0, NASA Exoplanet Archive; discovery and mass in Dai et al. 2017, AJ 154, 226). It is used only for the real-data check (`experiments.error_propagation.run_real_data_check("k2-131")`), fitted directly with no injected orbit. Our direct fit gives e≈0.13 rather than the literature's e≈0 (`results/real_data/summary.csv`). That is a single maximum-likelihood fit with no uncertainty attached, so we do not treat it as an eccentricity measurement. The point of the check is that all five solvers agree with each other on real data, which they do. It is not used for the tolerance sweep, since a near-circular orbit barely exercises the Kepler solver. | Fariha | 2026-09-24 |
